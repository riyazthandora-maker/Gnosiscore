Teacher / Parent

Educator
1
Register & wait for approval
Go to gnosiscore.org/register, choose Teacher / Parent, fill in your details. Admin will approve your account — you'll receive an email once approved.
2
Upload your study materials
From the Documents page, upload PDFs or images (max 4 MB each). The system converts them to searchable text automatically. Wait for status to show Ready.
PDF & images supported
3
Generate questions
Go to Generate, select your documents, enter a topic, choose difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) and number of questions. Questions are generated strictly from your uploaded content — no hallucinations.
≤20 questions: instant  ·  >20: needs admin approval
4
Review & approve questions
All generated questions land in Review. Read each question, edit if needed, then click Approve or Reject. Only approved questions enter your question bank.
5
Build a test
Go to Test Builder, pick questions from your bank, set a title and configure options. Save as draft or publish immediately.
6
Assign to students
Open the test, click Assign, enter student email addresses (one per line), optionally set a due date. Students receive an email with a direct link.
Email notification sent automatically
7
Track performance
View per-test and per-student analytics from the Analytics page. Export results as CSV anytime.
📚

Student

Student
1
Register your account
Go to gnosiscore.org/register, choose Student, enter your name, email and a password. Your account is activated immediately — no waiting for approval.
2
Log in
Go to gnosiscore.org/login. Enter your email and click Send OTP — a 6-digit code is emailed to you. Enter it to sign in. Alternatively use your password.
Check spam if code doesn't arrive
3
Open an assigned test
When a teacher assigns you a test, you'll receive an email with a Take Test button. Click it — or find the test in your Student Dashboard under Assigned Tests.
Email includes the due date
4
Answer the questions
Read each question and click the option you think is correct (A, B, C or D). Use the Previous / Next buttons to navigate. You can change any answer before submitting.
5
Submit & see your score
Click Submit Test when done. Your score appears immediately along with correct answers and explanations for every question — great for learning from mistakes.
6
Review past tests
All completed tests and scores are saved in your dashboard. You can revisit any result at any time to study the explanations again.
